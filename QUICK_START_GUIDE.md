## 🎓 How to Talk About This Project

### Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"I built an AI-powered Rock-Paper-Scissors game that uses computer vision to recognize hand gestures in real-time. The AI opponent learns from your playing patterns using a Markov Chain model to predict and counter your moves. It processes 30+ frames per second and gets smarter the more you play."

### Technical Description (2 minutes)
"The project combines computer vision and machine learning. For the vision component, I used OpenCV and MediaPipe to track hand landmarks and recognize gestures based on finger patterns. For the AI, I implemented a second-order Markov Chain that tracks sequences of moves to predict what the player will do next. The AI then counters the predicted move. I balanced this with an exploration strategy to keep gameplay engaging. The entire system runs in real-time at over 30 FPS."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't:** Just list technologies without explaining what you built
✅ **Do:** Explain the problem, your solution, and the impact

❌ **Don't:** Say "I used OpenCV" without details
✅ **Do:** Say "I implemented real-time gesture recognition using OpenCV's hand tracking at 30+ FPS"

❌ **Don't:** Claim you're an expert after one project
✅ **Do:** Show you understand the fundamentals and are eager to learn more

❌ **Don't:** Memorize scripts
✅ **Do:** Understand the concepts so you can discuss naturally
